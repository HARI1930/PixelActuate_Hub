Frequently Asked Questions (Principals & Parents)

Clear answers on logistics, safety, and academic alignment

1. Does the school need to purchase Arduino kits, sensors, or laptops?

No—zero infrastructure expenditure is required. PixelActuate Hub brings all microcontrollers, breadboards, sensors, motors, and jumper kits. We only use 4 desktops in your existing computer lab for 60 minutes on Saturday.

2. How does a 4-week bootcamp fit into school exam schedules?

Sessions run for just 60 minutes on Saturdays (typically during the last period or activity slot) and conclude cleanly in 4 weeks—reinforcing Physics electricity/sound and Math logic right before exams.

3. Are the electronics safe for Class 6–10 students?

Yes, 100%. All classroom circuits operate on low-voltage 5V DC USB power with zero high-voltage AC connections and no soldering irons required.
