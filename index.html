Official Bootcamp Curriculum & Roadmap

🗓️ 4-Week Engineering Roadmap (60 Mins / Session)

  • Week 1 — Circuit Foundations & Logic: Current flow, breadboard wiring, resistors, and LED control logic.
  • Week 2 — Touchless Sensors & Alerts: Ultrasonic echo timing and IR obstacle detection systems.
  • Week 3 — Robotic Actuation & Kinematics: PWM signals and precision micro-servo motor control.
  • Week 4 — Smart City Automation Finale: Building an automated emergency barrier gate + dual-signed Principal & Founder certificate.

🏫 Zero-Infrastructure Model for Schools

  • ₹0 School Investment: We bring all Arduino microcontrollers, sensors, motors, and breadboards.

  • Also Available: Weekend batches & Class 6–10 Math/Physics conceptual tuitions.
